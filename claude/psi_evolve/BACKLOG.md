## Explicitly NOT on the backlog

- **Broad architecture search.** The bake-off spread among six architectures was ~15% MAE against
  a ~2.5x gap to the trivial baselines. AR-02 is in because the TCN result replicated across two
  horizons, not as an opening for a search.
- **More simulations downstream.** Measured inert (DS-03).
- **Within-subset weight tuning.** Uniform ~ saturated within 0.008 R2, and skill *decreases* as
  the likelihood concentrates. A 0.008-R2 lever.
